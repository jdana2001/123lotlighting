  • Wall packs over doors and along building walls

Repair or replace

If a wall pack is out, we fix it. If it's an old metal halide unit, it's often worth replacing with an LED wall pack instead: they cost little more than a major repair and last far longer.

Rebates for LED upgrades. If your building is a ComEd customer, replacing older fixtures with LED may qualify for an incentive through the ComEd Energy Efficiency Program, which can take a real bite out of the cost. Ask when you request your quote and we'll help you check. Naperville runs its own electric utility, so Naperville properties should check with the city.

Frequently asked questions

What is a wall pack?

A wall pack is a light mounted on the side of a building, usually over doors, along walkways and at loading docks. Older ones use metal halide or sodium lamps; new ones are LED.

How much does an LED wall pack cost?

$350–$550 per wall pack installed, and $250–$400 to repair one that's out.

Can you add lights where it's dark now?

Often, yes, where there's power nearby. Send photos of the area and we'll tell you what it takes.
