Frequently asked questions

A car knocked over our light pole. What do we do?

Keep people and cars away from the base, since the wiring may still be live. Call us and we'll make it safe, remove the pole and quote the replacement. If the pole was hit by a driver, the replacement is often a claim on their insurance, so keep the police report or incident details.

How much does it cost to replace a light pole?

Replacing a damaged pole on its existing base runs $3,150–$5,250, including rehanging the fixture. A cracked or broken concrete base adds to that, and we quote it once we've seen it.

Is a leaning or rusted pole dangerous?

It can be. Rust at the base and loose anchor bolts weaken a pole, and wind or a light bump can bring it down. Send photos of the base and we'll take a look.
