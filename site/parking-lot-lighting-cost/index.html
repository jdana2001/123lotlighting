What changes the price

  • Number of fixtures. The biggest factor. Fixing several lights on one visit spreads the lift and trip cost.
  • Repair vs. LED. Repairs cost less now. LED costs more up front but far less to run and maintain.
  • Pole height and access. Very tall poles or poles in landscaping need bigger lifts.
  • Wiring problems. A whole row out can mean underground wiring, which is quoted after we find the fault.
  • Rebates. ComEd incentives can lower the net cost of LED upgrades for eligible buildings.

Frequently asked questions

How much does parking lot light repair cost?

Fixing a light that's out runs $250–$400 per fixture, including the lift. Replacing a pole-mounted head with an LED fixture is $700–$1,200, and an LED wall pack is $350–$550. Lighting jobs have a $495 minimum, which covers the lift and the electrician's trip. See parking lot lighting cost for examples.

Why is there a minimum charge?

Every lighting job needs a lift and an electrician on site, even for one light. The $495 minimum covers that trip. Fixing several lights on one visit is much cheaper per light.

Is it cheaper to repair or replace with LED?

A repair is $250–$400 per fixture and an LED head is $700–$1,200. For one or two lights, repair. For a lot full of aging metal halide fixtures, LED usually wins over a few years once power savings, fewer repairs and any ComEd incentive are counted.

Does the price include the lift?

Yes. Repair and fixture prices include the lift or bucket truck. Pole replacement includes rehanging the fixture.
