Parking lot lighting questions

How much does parking lot light repair cost?

Fixing a light that's out runs $250–$400 per fixture, including the lift. Replacing a pole-mounted head with an LED fixture is $700–$1,200, and an LED wall pack is $350–$550. Lighting jobs have a $495 minimum, which covers the lift and the electrician's trip. See parking lot lighting cost for examples.

Why do parking lot lights go out?

On older metal halide and high-pressure sodium fixtures it's usually a worn-out lamp or a failed ballast. On LED fixtures it's usually the driver. A bad photocell or timer can keep a whole row dark, and damaged underground wiring can knock out several poles at once. Lights that cycle on and off are a classic sign of a lamp or ballast at the end of its life.

Is it worth upgrading parking lot lights to LED?

Usually, yes. LED fixtures often use half the power or less of the metal halide heads they replace, last far longer, and spread light more evenly, so the lot looks brighter with fewer dark spots. If your building is a ComEd customer, the upgrade may qualify for an incentive through the ComEd Energy Efficiency Program. (Naperville runs its own electric utility.)

Do you work at night?

Most repairs and upgrades are done during the day. Lights are tested by covering the photocell, so we can see which ones work without coming back after dark.

Do you have to close the parking lot?

No. The lift takes up a stall or two around each pole. We cone off that area while we work and move on, so the rest of the lot stays open.

Why are my lot lights on during the day, or off too early at night?

That's almost always a failed photocell or a timer that's set wrong or lost its settings after a power outage. It's a quick fix, and it stops you paying to light an empty lot at noon.

How do I get a quote?

Use the quote form with the address, how many lights you have and how many are out. Photos help. We email a written, fixed-price quote, usually within one business day, and confirm each fixture on site before any work.

Can you fix the pavement and lines at the same time?

Yes. Our sister companies 123 Pothole Repair and 123 Lot Striping fill potholes, seal cracks, sealcoat and restripe. Add it all on one quote form and get one price.
